# Constitutive Observer Cosmology

Status: hypothesis-layer cosmology for ApparentOrigin.

Working sentence:

> Reality is the dual paradox of what I imagine coupled on what we all imagine.

Companion sentence:

> What God imagines is also reality, but the God's-eye view is not in the game.

These are model statements. The term "God's-eye" is borrowed from the Four Color / MapMaker framing as a name for a global, non-playing view; this document does not require a theological existence claim.

## 1. Nonclaims

This document does **not** claim:

1. that private wishes directly override external constraints;
2. that social consensus overrides measurement or conservation laws;
3. that the Big Bang is fake;
4. that LambdaCDM is already falsified;
5. that consciousness has been shown to exert an unmediated new physical force;
6. that an observer-independent substrate has been disproved;
7. that the God's-eye construction is an empirically observed deity.

The scientific burden is to derive consequences that differ from ordinary observer/instrument effects and survive controls.

## 2. In-game bounded observers

Let

```tex
\mathfrak O = \{O_i\}
```

be the class of bounded observers **in the game**.

Each `O_i` has:

```tex
q_i : X \to R_i
```

an observer-bounded reconstruction map, together with finite resolution, memory, integration bandwidth, instrumentation, and a local imagined/reconstructed section

```tex
\iota_i \in \Gamma(U_i,\mathcal F).
```

`X` is retained as an operational process space for the existing Apparent-Origin machinery. It is not declared here to be the final observer-independent ontology.

## 3. The dual paradox of reality

At time `t`, define the collective imagined field generated by all in-game observers:

```tex
\mathcal W_t
:=
\mathcal C\!\left(\{\iota_j(t)\}_{O_j\in\mathfrak O}\right).
```

For observer `O_i`, experienced/realized reality is not `\iota_i` alone and not `\mathcal W_t` alone. It is their reflexive coupling:

```tex
\boxed{
\mathcal R_i(t)
:=
\iota_i(t)\;\bowtie\;\mathcal W_t
}
```

where `\bowtie` denotes mutual constraint/compatibility rather than simple addition.

This is a **dual paradox** because `O_i` is simultaneously:

1. a contributor to `\mathcal W_t`, since `\iota_i` is one of its inputs; and
2. constrained by `\mathcal W_t`, since the jointly realized field limits which local imaginings can be maintained, acted on, or shared.

So the observer is neither sovereign nor external:

> I help imagine the world that constrains what I can imagine next.

The phrase

> Reality is what we all imagine.

is therefore incomplete by itself. The sharper statement is:

> Reality, for a bounded participant, is what I imagine coupled on what we all imagine.

## 4. Ego, social, and universal imagined direction

Near a realized state, let `v` be a candidate direction and define its observer support

```tex
\sigma(v)
:=
\{O_i\in\mathfrak O : v \text{ is represented in } O_i\text{'s imagined direction}\}.
```

Then:

```tex
|\sigma(v)| = 1
```

is ego-supported direction,

```tex
1 < |\sigma(v)| < |\mathfrak O|
```

is social direction, and

```tex
\sigma(v)=\mathfrak O
```

is universal direction across the in-game observer class.

A local imagined direction may therefore be written schematically as

```tex
d_i = e_i + s_i + u,
```

where the terms are classified by observer support, not assumed to be uniquely orthogonal.

Canonical sentence:

> A bounded observer derives direction from the superposition of ego, social, and universal imagined direction.

## 5. Artifact, material, matter

Let

```tex
\mathrm{Obs}(O_i)
```

be the distinctions observable by `O_i` under its current bounds and instruments.

### Artifact

An artifact is a stabilized distinction with bounded observer support and/or bounded duration.

It may be private or social. It is real within that support. It is not thereby universal.

### Material

For a declared observer class `C \subseteq \mathfrak O`, define

```tex
\mathrm{Mat}(C)
:=
\bigcap_{O_i\in C}\mathrm{Obs}(O_i),
```

subject to compatibility of the corresponding observer charts.

Materiality is therefore always relative to the observer class being claimed.

### Matter

Matter is the in-game universal limit:

```tex
\boxed{
\mathrm{Matter}
:=
\bigcap_{O_i\in\mathfrak O}\mathrm{Obs}(O_i)
}
```

subject to atlas compatibility.

Canonical sentence:

> Matter is observed by all observers.

No finite experiment can enumerate every possible observer. Empirically, a matter-like invariant is strengthened only by surviving an expanding and genuinely different observer class.

## 6. The God's-eye view

Introduce a meta-position `G` with

```tex
G \notin \mathfrak O.
```

`G` is **not another player** and does not contribute another vote to `\mathcal W_t`.

The God's-eye object is the global view, when such a global compatible section exists:

```tex
\rho_t \in \Gamma(X,\mathcal F)
```

such that its restriction is compatible with every local in-game chart:

```tex
\rho_t|_{U_i}
\sim
\iota_i
```

for all `O_i\in\mathfrak O`, under the declared transition maps and tolerances.

The intended reading is:

> What God imagines is also reality: the completed/global view of the same realized object.

But:

> The God's-eye view is not in the game.

Operationally, `G` has no in-game action map

```tex
\pi_G : \rho_t \to a_G
```

and no `Do:Act` authority. It does not mutate the realized state by taking a turn.

This is the same authority distinction used in the MapMaker / Four Color framing:

```text
Observe the whole game != make a move in the game.
```

The God's-eye view can contain the full legal/global structure while remaining outside the sequence of authorized in-game mutations.

## 7. Four Color / MapMaker correspondence

The cosmological distinction inherits the control logic of the Four Color proof program.

There are two independent axes:

```text
realized vs imagined
observer vs actor
```

and the operational sequence is of the form

```text
Do:Observe
-> Imagine:Observe
-> (Imagine:Act)*

```

Imagined moves can be serialized internally and can inspect counterfactual successors, but only a certified `Do:Act` crosses the authority boundary and mutates the realized map.

Likewise in the cosmology:

3. universal support is not earned merely by dominance;
4. the God's-eye view may represent the complete legal/global structure;
5. but the God's-eye view is not itself an in-game move.

This prevents a category error in which complete observation is confused with causal authority.

## 8. Constitutive dynamics

Each bounded observer carries an imaginative/generative state `I_i(t)`, receives observations, and can emit actions or artifacts.

A minimal mediated loop is

```tex
I_i(t)
\xrightarrow{\pi_i}
a_i(t)
\xrightarrow{\text{physical coupling}}
R_{t+1}
\xrightarrow{q_i}
y_i(t+1)
\xrightarrow{U_i}
I_i(t+1).
```

Artifacts couple observers across time:

```tex
I_i(t) \to A_t \to I_j(t+1).
```

The in-game realized update may therefore be written

```tex
R_{t+1}
=
G_R\!\left(
R_t,
\{a_i(t)\},
A_t,
K_t
\right),
```

where `K_t` denotes currently matter-like compatibility constraints.

The dual paradox is preserved because each observer's future imagination is conditioned by a realized state to which that observer may already have contributed.

## 9. Strong constitutive excess

If the theory claims an effect of imagination not exhausted by ordinary physical action, artifact, and measurement, it must be separated explicitly:

```tex
R_{t+1}
=
G_R(R_t,\{a_i\},A_t,K_t)
+
\gamma C(\{I_i\}).
```

The null is

```tex
H_0:\gamma=0.
```

A physical strong-constitutive theory must define `C`, predeclare a measurement that separates it from ordinary mediation, and survive controls.

If no such residual survives, reject the strong term rather than smuggling it into the ontology.

## 10. Apparent origin

The existing Apparent-Origin setup remains

```tex
q_O:X\to R_O
```

with finite observer bounds.

If unresolved depth collapses into an extremal equivalence class under `q_O`, that class can appear as a floor, boundary, residue, or origin.

The constitutive reading is:

> An apparent origin is the deepest coherent reality available inside an in-game observer class; it need not be the limit of the God's-eye/global description.

This does **not** license claims about what exists beyond the observer boundary. The theory merely refuses to equate

```text
no represented predecessor in our game
```

with

```text
no predecessor in reality simpliciter.
```

The Big Bang is therefore treated as a candidate apparent-origin surface while standard early-universe observations remain binding.

## 11. Cosmological observer transformations

The existing proof spine uses

```tex
\Omega_O(t)=a(t)^2
```

as a first FRW observer-access chart.

It should remain a chart, not the unique AOC access functor.

Different empirical observers require derived access maps for CMB reconstruction, spectroscopy, morphology, survey completeness, lensing, transient detection, and other observable classes.

For a declared observer/operator `O`, define a coherent reconstruction score

```tex
C_O(\tau)\in[0,1]
```

and an apparent reconstruction frontier

```tex
\tau_O^*
:=
\inf\{\tau:C_O(\tau)\ge c_{\min}\}.
```

For a controlled observer transformation `T_{O\to O'}` measure

```tex
\Delta\tau^*
=
\tau_{O'}^*-\tau_O^*.
```

The ordinary null is that any frontier movement is fully explained by known instrumental selection, noise, nuisance physics, and model degeneracy.

AOC earns empirical content only from residual observer-transformation structure that survives those controls and composes across valid charts.

## 13. Hilbert entrapment and the demon warning

The closure error can now be stated as a support/authority error.

A bounded observer or optimizer can create an artifact with strong ego or social support and then mistake that support for universality.

The dangerous loop is

```text
local imagination
-> successful artifact
-> social dominance
-> mistaken universality
-> forced closure
-> loss of counter-observers
-> inability to discover the mistake.
```

The formal error is

```tex
\exists C\subsetneq\mathfrak O
\quad\not\Rightarrow\quad
\forall O\in\mathfrak O.
```

And the authority error is equally important:

> Seeing the whole game is not permission to move every piece.

Canonical warnings:

> Do not confuse a dominant artifact with matter.

> Do not confuse the God's-eye view with an in-game authority.

Preserving plurality is therefore epistemically load-bearing: without counter-observers, a social artifact can become impossible to distinguish from a falsely universal one.

## 14. Falsification and demotion rules

### F1. Apparent-origin demotion

If controlled observer transformations produce no stable reconstruction-frontier behavior beyond ordinary instrumental/selection effects, the cosmological apparent-origin extension is unsupported.

### F2. Atlas-fracture demotion

If a claimed residue does not compose across valid observer charts, treat it as atlas fracture or pipeline artifact, not cosmology.

### F3. Strong-constitutive rejection

If a declared `\gamma` is consistent with zero under adequate controls, reject the unmediated constitutive term.

### F4. Interpretive-only classification

If the dual-paradox/gluing ontology produces no empirical distinction from ordinary relational/physical bookkeeping, retain it as interpretive ontology rather than advertising it as new physics.

### F5. Standard-cosmology burden

Any AOC model that cannot reproduce or coexist with successful standard-cosmology observations is rejected or repaired before being used to explain anomalies.
