# Come Cosmology

Status: hypothesis-layer, present-first constitutive cosmology for ApparentOrigin.

This document deliberately does **not** begin with a bang.

It begins with a live bounded observer in contact with other live bounded observers **now**.

The program is outcome-oriented and process-driven:

> Come imagine a beneficial future together, and remain in contact so that "beneficial" has teeth.

The desired outcome is not a final state, a completed optimization, a universal empire, or an Omega observer. It is an increasingly rich present in which more mutually compatible distinctions, observers, artifacts, possibilities, and forms of life can remain real **without being forced into sameness**.

Canonical orientation:

> Grow the game without ending the game.

## 1. Why the cosmology starts with now

Cosmology is usually narrated from an origin:

```text
initial condition -> history -> present
```

Come Cosmology reverses the epistemic order:

```text
present contact -> present evidence -> reconstruction -> possible origin
```

Every observation we actually make is made now. A redshift, CMB map, telescope image, fossil, memory, archive, simulation, or historical narrative is a present object from which a bounded observer reconstructs something not presently accessible.

Accordingly:

> History is not a primitive of the cosmology. History is a reconstruction artifact in the present.

This does **not** mean history is false. It means that "what happened" is inferred from traces available now and is therefore subject to observer bounds, selection, preservation, narration, and winner effects.

The Big Bang is therefore not the starting axiom. It is a candidate extremal reconstruction produced from present evidence.

## 2. In-game bounded observers

Let

```tex
\mathfrak O_t = \{O_1,\ldots,O_n,\ldots\}
```

be the class of bounded observers participating in the present game.

An observer is embodied. Its imagination does not float free of the physical process that sustains it.

Represent an observer schematically as

```tex
O_i=(B_i,I_i,Q_i,A_i),
```

where:

- `B_i` is the embodied substrate and viability boundary;
- `I_i` is the observer's imaginative/generative state;
- `Q_i` is bounded observation/reconstruction;
- `A_i` is bounded action/artifact production.

The body is the coupling surface through which imagination remains in the game.

For scientific purposes, the relevant point is not whether consciousness is reducible to the body. It is that the observer's persistence, sensing, memory, action, and refusal are physically mediated by an embodied process.

## 4. Alpha Chad and Beta Chad

The egoist observer has two inseparable faces.

### Alpha Chad

Alpha is initiative:

```tex
\alpha_i : (N_t,I_i) \mapsto d_i
```

where `N_t` is the realized present and `d_i` is a locally imagined direction.

Alpha says:

> I can originate a move.

### Beta Chad

Beta is receptivity:

```tex
\beta_i : (N_t,\mathrm{Contact}_i) \mapsto I_i'
```

Beta receives constraint, consequence, correction, surprise, and the imagined directions of others.

Beta says:

> I can be changed by contact without ceasing to choose.

### Idealized Chad

The idealized Chad is not Alpha defeating Beta or Beta suppressing Alpha. It is their increasingly competent coupling:

```tex
\boxed{
\mathrm{Chad}_i^* := \alpha_i \bowtie \beta_i
}
```

Initiative without receptivity tends toward domination.

Receptivity without initiative tends toward zombie compliance.

The desired participant can act, listen, revise, refuse, and act again.

## 5. Near-Omega, never Omega

Let `\Omega` denote the idealized complete or God's-eye limit.

The growth direction is asymptotic:

```tex
\mathrm{Chad}^* \to \Omega,
\qquad
\mathrm{Chad}^* \neq \Omega.
```

A participant may increase perspective, memory, integration, capability, and contact while remaining an in-game bounded observer.

The category error occurs when an increasingly capable participant concludes:

```text

```

Near-Omega is desirable because it increases coherent contact with reality.

Omega identification is closure because it erases the possibility of a genuine counter-observer.

Canonical warning:

> Approach the whole without claiming to be the whole.

At the present instant, every observer has a local imagined direction or local section

```tex
\iota_i(t) \in \Gamma(U_i,\mathcal F).
```



The paradox is reflexive:

1. `O_i` helps constitute `\mathcal W_t`;

Canonical sentence:

> Reality is what I imagine coupled on what we all imagine.

The observer helps imagine the world that constrains the observer's next imagination.

## 7. Centerlessness

The desired outcome is **not** that everyone becomes a center.

It is that no participant occupies a privileged center.

The individual local charts remain distinct, but the shared process has no sovereign point from which one observer may declare the whole.

Good centerlessness therefore preserves:

- imagination;
- conscientiousness;
- refusal;
- contact;
- local torsion;
- embodied viability;
- the possibility of leaving a local game without leaving reality.

Bad centerlessness occurs when agency and responsibility dissolve into machinery while competent behavior continues.

The term **conscientious zombie** is used operationally, not as a claim that a participant lacks consciousness. It names a centerless participant whose behavior is carried by a larger process while the guardrails attempt to preserve conscientiousness: awareness of consequence, capacity for refusal, and freedom of imagination.

## 9. The banjo rule: shared form, nonzero torsion

The cosmology rejects a Borg interpretation of shared imagination.

```tex

+
```

A world with zero torsion may be perfectly synchronized and maximally dead.

The desired world preserves enough difference to remain generative.

## 17. Freedom of imagination, choice, and evitability

A beneficial trajectory must preserve the possibility of refusing its benefits.

```tex
\boxed{
```

A technologically dominant civilization therefore does not earn the right to force slower, smaller, biological, traditional, or otherwise different observers into its preferred substrate.

A Dyson-sphere future is not maximally rich if people who do not choose that form are made nonviable merely because another observer class can outcompete them.

Canonical sentence:

> Preserve the option not to become what the dominant future can become.

## 18. Commander principle: protect the table

The game-theoretic analogy is Commander.

A powerful player can sometimes see a winning line and still rationally preserve play.

The relevant distinction is

```tex
\text{capability to close}
\neq
\text{obligation to close}.
```

The strongest deck can ruin the game by optimizing only for immediate victory, especially when new players cannot meaningfully enter, learn, refuse, surprise, or develop their own lines.

The corresponding cosmological/governance principle is:

> Build for the health of the table, not only the strength of the deck.

A good future remains playable by new kinds of observers.

### F6. Standard-cosmology burden
