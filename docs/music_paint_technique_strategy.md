# Music / Paint Technique Strategy

Status: living strategy document. Not procedure, not validation rubric.

This document records the strategic frame under which AOC imports
musical and pictorial technique as method (not decoration), the
contingency stack that bounds the program, and the sprint candidates
that operationalize the import.

Read after `AGENTS.md`. Pointers at the bottom for the canonical
documents this strategy sits inside.

## Strategic frame

The goal is not "AOC is true" — it is for AOC to dominate **on multiple
independent axes** such that any single wobble does not collapse the
program. Lakatos protective belt, not single-thread-of-truth.

Even "best in class" is conditional on a stack of *ifs*:
*if* the conjecture is true, *if* it is verifiable on our planet alone
ever, *if* it is verifiable at all, *if* verifiability itself is more
than a social construct. Verifiability-as-social-construct is itself an
AOC-adjacent claim, not a side gripe; observer-bounded measurement does
not promise observer-independent confirmation. These are bets, not
guarantees.

## Classes of dominance

| class | what wins here | technique-import value |
|---|---|---|
| **empirical** | numerical predictions that hit + survive sim nulls | indirect — frames *which* tests to run |
| **methodological** | tools other cosmologists adopt regardless of theory | high — sonification, voice-leading, prismatic decomposition |
| **communication** | the work is inspectable without dumbing down | high — Opticks layer is the first move |
| **epistemological** | the frame (observer-bounded, geometricity-from-measurement) is coherent and productive | demonstrative — paint/music importing cleanly *is* evidence the primitive cuts something |
| **compositional** | integrates with Friston / Wheeler / Pathria-Good without dissolving | direct — counterpoint as model for hierarchical predictive processing |

Each class is independently defensible. AOC can be best-in-class on any
one without committing the others. The protective belt is the union,
not a chain.

## The technique-import primitive

Music and paint technique are crystallized **observer-discipline** —
centuries of refining how a bounded apparatus renders high-dimensional
structure without flattening it. Cosmology has Fisher matrices and
Gaussian likelihoods; it does not have counterpoint, voice-leading,
color-grade discipline, sonification grammar, or Newtonian prismatic
decomposition as method.

The reason this import is honest rather than analogical, in AOC:
**geometricity originates from observer measurement** (see
`memory/project_geometricity_from_observation.md` in the auto-memory
layer). Color, sound, and angular alignment all share circular structure
because all three are bounded-observer measurement traces. They are
projections of one observer-substrate body, not three independent
domains that happen to share math. So importing the technique-stack
that has spent the longest on observer-rendering is inheriting the
right tool.

This is a hard-core commitment, not a protective-belt move.

**Sprint A** is the canonical first move because it demonstrates the
import-strategy is real method, not decoration. **Sprint D** is the arc
target — the piece that, if it lands, gives empirical-class evidence,
not just methodological contribution.

## Discipline carry-forward

The technique-import does not relax the standing project disciplines.
In particular:

- **Phase respect** (gesture/near-cousin/instantiation/publication)
  applies to imported technique as well. Counterpoint rules adapted for
  sphere geometry are *gesture* on first pass; they become
  *instantiation* only when their predictions are committed in advance
  and the data tests them.
- **Drift naming** applies. If counterpoint vocabulary smuggles in
  premises that don't survive translation to the sphere, name it.
- **Confidence quantification** on every structural-similarity claim.
  Especially for cross-modal isomorphisms — the S¹ piece is faithful;
  the S² piece compresses; say which is which and at what confidence.
- **Allowed/forbidden claims** in every artifact. The renderings are
  inspection layers, not new evidence. Sonification with sharper
  perceptual discrimination still encodes the same data.
