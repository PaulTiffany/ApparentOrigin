# Glossary

Status: canonical vocabulary for this repository.

## Apparent-Origin Cosmology

The research program studying whether the earliest reconstructible cosmological
boundary should be interpreted as an observer-bounded reconstruction horizon
rather than an automatic ontological beginning.

## Apparent-Origin Surface

A boundary in an observer's reconstruction that appears origin-like because
unresolved depth has been quotiented into an extremal equivalence class.

## False Bottom

Public metaphor for an apparent-origin surface. Use carefully. It does not mean
"the Big Bang is fake." It means the reconstruction may bottom out before being
reaches the ontological bottom.

## Reconstruction Horizon

Physics-facing term for the deepest coherent surface available to a bounded
observer or observation pipeline.

## Observer Horizon

Principia-facing term for the dynamic boundary of a situated observer. An
observer horizon is not only a measurement cutoff; it is the operating domain
within which memory, instrumentation, drift, reflection, and curvature shape
what can be reconstructed.

## Escape Horizon

AOC-facing term for the first surface from which a given observer class can
recover coherent observables. The Big Bang boundary is treated as a candidate
escape horizon, not as a directly denied event or a directly inspected
pre-boundary state.

## Dual-Horizon Cosmogenesis

Principia model class in which an observable domain is structured between a
generative boundary and a dissipative boundary. AOC may use this as a
hypothesis generator, but it is not a physical claim until a process space,
observer quotient, reconstruction order, and observable consequence are
specified.

## Observer-Bounded Reconstruction

A map from an underlying process space to an observer's reconstruction space
under finite resolution, memory, integration bandwidth, instrumentation, and
model capacity.

## Observer Quotient

The equivalence relation induced when distinctions below the observer threshold
cannot be maintained. Unresolved variation is represented as an equivalence
class rather than as further depth.

## Observer Thickness

The effective capacity of an observer to preserve, stage, and integrate
distinctions before emitting a reconstruction.

Thin observers expose boundaries sharply. Thick observers amortize boundaries
across memory, embodiment, instruments, tools, culture, retrieval, scratchpads,
or staged computation.

Canonical sentence:

> Observer thickness determines whether a boundary appears as a cliff or as
> smooth judgment.

## Amortization

The relocation of reconstruction cost from an exposed act into prior
integration, memory, tooling, instrumentation, culture, or staged computation.

Canonical sentence:

> Amortization does not eliminate the obstruction; it pays the cost elsewhere.

## Atlas Fracture

The failure mode where an observer's local reconstruction charts no longer
compose coherently under drift. Apparent-origin claims are legitimate only when
the reconstruction atlas remains coherent up to the boundary.

## K Parameter

The finite reconstruction budget used in the first FRW observer-quotient chart.
In that chart, `K` bounds the access cost:

```tex
\kappa_O^{access}(t) \le K.
```

Until `K` is derived or estimated from apparatus, coarse-graining, or a physical
bound, AOC remains interpretive rather than predictive.

## Apparatus-Bound K

The safest current framing of `K`: the effective budget of a concrete
instrument and inference pipeline.

## RG-Effective K

A possible future framing in which `K` runs under coarse-graining. This remains
a placeholder until a real running equation is derived.

## Fundamental K

A possible future framing in which `K` is constrained by fundamental physical
capacity, such as entropy or holographic bounds. This is deferred.

## Feasibility Band

The range where an observer has enough reliable access to operate, instrument,
compose, and analyze without pretending to be unbounded.

Canonical sentence:

> Compose only where the operator remains measurable.

## Pivot Regime

A minority out-of-contract band where the current routing or reconstruction
contract fails to preserve coherence, but the failure is structured enough to
force recomposition rather than rejection. It is not random exploration, not a
universal constant, and not mere uncertainty. It marks residue that cannot be
routed by smooth continuation under the current chart. Numerical markers such
as `1/9` may recur as empirical signs of a contract boundary, but they are not
the definition of the regime.

Canonical sentence:

> Bounded intelligence metabolizes out-of-contract residue into a new routing
> contract without losing closure.

## Operation, Instrumentation, Composition, Analysis

The repository's practical feasibility chain: SRMF expressed inside the
feasibility band.

1. **Operation / TTDC:** what can act or be acted on.
2. **Instrumentation / TTIE:** what distinction can be measured or interfaced.
3. **Composition / TTCS:** what carries and integrates coherently across operators,
   charts, observers, or stages without losing closure.
4. **Analysis / TTPR:** what can be inspected, compared, controlled, or falsified.

Canonical sentence:

> Explicit means stabilized by shared operation, instrumentation, composition,
> and analysis.

## Authorship of Bounds

The agency reading of the framework: an agent is not free because it lacks
constraints, but because it can inspect, revise, stage, and author the bounds
under which it reconstructs.

## Second-Order Boundary Evidence

The kind of empirical evidence AOC can legitimately seek. Since a
reconstruction horizon is not directly inspectable from behind, AOC evidence
must come from boundary behavior, pipeline-dependent reconstruction floors,
stable inter-probe mismatch patterns, or control-resistant residuals.

Canonical sentence:

> AOC is testable only through boundary behavior, not through direct inspection
> of what its own model places beyond the observer horizon.

## Cosmological Gabriel's Horn

Hypothesis-generator metaphor for a larger cosmological structure in which our
Big Bang is an observer-dependent cross-section or apparent floor rather than
the unique absolute beginning. This is not a claim until a process space,
observer quotient, reconstruction order, and second-order observable consequence
are specified.

## Operator Residue

The boundary-adjacent structure that remains, changes, or disappears when the
same target is reconstructed by different operators. Operator residue is a
cleaner near-term empirical target for AOC than trying to export one universal
distance deformation across all probes.

## Observable-Feasibility Kernel

A theory-side closed-form expression that says what structure is *admissible*
under a near-cousin geometry plus a finite reconstruction budget, before any
dataset is consulted. The Episode 4 example is the Kerr horizon kernel
`h_K(chi) = (1 - sqrt(1 - chi^2)) / (1 + sqrt(1 - chi^2))` and the admissible
scale `Lambda_K^adm(chi, K_P) = h_K(chi) / K_P`. A feasibility kernel is
*not* an observed amplitude; the observable / observed type guard applies.

## Observable / Observed Type Guard

The discipline that distinguishes three levels: *feasible* (allowed by the
near-cousin geometry), *observable* (survives a specified apparatus / pipeline
feasibility band), and *observed* (estimated in a dataset). The forbidden
shortcut is treating a feasibility kernel as if it were an observed amplitude.

## Channel-Response Coefficient

Written `C_P`. The signed coefficient that maps an observable-feasibility
scale into one specified observable channel under one specified prism. Until
`C_P` is predeclared (sign at minimum, amplitude eventually), a feasibility
kernel does not yield a dataset-facing prediction. Choosing `C_P` *after*
seeing the target dataset is hidden channel fitting and is forbidden by the
SRMF invariant card.

## Prismatic Decomposition

The Sprint E robustness grammar for an operator-residue feature. A feature is
asked which decomposition prisms it survives, which prism creates or destroys
it, and which prism recomposes it. The three canonical prisms are the
*multipole prism* (single-ell vs band vs leakage), the *mask / sky-partition
prism* (unmasked vs synthetic galcut family vs official Planck mask
morphology), and the *operator prism* (Commander, NILC, SEVEM, SMICA, plus
pairwise residue and per-operator cross-octave behavior).

## Feature-State Labels

Routing labels (not honorifics) used to carry an operator-residue feature
forward through prismatic decomposition: *survives a prism*, *collapses
under a prism*, *recomposes*, *operator-bound* (lives in reconstruction
operators, not yet sky ontology), *mask-bound*, *shared-target residue
candidate* (survives enough operator and mask variation to deserve ordinary
CMB controls), *theory-contact candidate* (clean enough for a future
prediction to specify).

## Operator-Prism Coordinate

The judge-free scalar `C_axis(ell, m) = (D_res - D_op) / D_iso` used in the
Episode 4 Planck operator-prism contract. `D_op` is the median pairwise
axial angle among the four operator axes (n=4 -> 6 pairs). `D_res` is the
median pairwise axial angle among the six pair-residue axes (n=6 -> 15
pairs). `D_iso` is the isotropic n=4 axial-median reference, calibrated by
Sprint F1 to ~60 deg from first principles; the operator-prism gate uses
the predeclared 57 deg.

## CI LLM Compute Leverage

The methodology pattern by which a CI-side LLM agent stages GitHub-hosted
Linux compute for an operation that does not run in the local environment
(e.g., `healpy` extraction on Windows). Predeclaration discipline runs
forward: the contract is frozen in the local repo before the workflow
runs. Large data inputs are treated as fetched data, not Git-tracked
source. The pattern is a tool choice, not a delegation of authorship.

## Pipeline Independence Postulate

The standard reading in CMB component-separation practice that the four Planck
PR3 algorithms (Commander, NILC, SEVEM, SMICA) constitute four substantially
independent reconstructions of the cleaned CMB sky, and that their agreement
on a low-ell observable is therefore evidence of pipeline-independent recovery
rather than shared structure. AOC's Episode 2 voice-leading analysis flagged
that under masking transitions the four pipelines move in lockstep at ell=3
(parallel-fifths in 6/6 pairs), suggesting their agreement may be mask-driven
rather than four independent reconstructions converging. The postulate is the
field's default assumption that this work is testing, not a claim AOC endorses
or rejects without further calibration. Term coined externally (NotebookLM
Strategic Program Proposal output, 2026-04-28).
