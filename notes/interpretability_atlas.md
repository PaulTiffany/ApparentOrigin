# The AOC Interpretability Atlas: A Guide to the Science of the Interface

## 1. The Core Intuition: The Universe as an Atlas
Imagine the universe is a vast territory, and astronomers are cartographers. 
- **The Territory:** The physical substrate (the "real" universe).
- **The Atlas:** Our collection of data-driven maps (SNIa, BAO, CMB).

**The Problem:** Cartographers often assume their pens have comparable
precision at every scale. AOC asks when that assumption fails and how the
failure can be measured.

## 2. The Discovery Path (The "Cracks" in the Map)

### [The Density Catastrophe](./density_catastrophe.md)
The working hypothesis is that our "pens" run out of ink at high redshift.
Even if individual data points are sharp, the **sampling density** can collapse.
We use **Shannon Horizon** as a candidate metaphor for this reconstruction
stress.

### [The Spectral Plateau](./spectral_plateau_report.md)
The spectral diagnostic suggests a candidate saturation region near
**z=0.5**, where adding high-z data contributes less independent information
than row counts alone would suggest.

### [The Atlas Fracture](./differential_geometry_report.md)
If the map saturates near z=0.5, the "Low-z" and "High-z" partitions may no
longer stitch cleanly. In the current diagnostics they prefer different
effective parameters. We call this candidate mismatch an **Atlas Fracture**.

## 3. The Mechanical Solution: H0 as Information Pressure

### [The Information Pressure Law](./information_pressure_mechanism.md)
This is the program's candidate mechanism. The current notes test whether the
**Hubble Tension** can be modeled as an effective "pressure" required to keep
information flow consistent across a candidate fracture.
- **High Information Flow (Local):** High Pressure ($H_0 \approx 73$).
- **Low Information Flow (Global):** Low Pressure ($H_0 \approx 67$).

The 9% tension may be interpretable as a stitching residue between a
high-resolution local map and a lower-resolution global map. That interpretation
requires controls before it can be treated as physical mechanism.

## 4. Why This Matters (Anti-Woo Summary)
AOC tries to move apparent-origin questions from **metaphysics** toward
**metrology**.
1. **The Big Bang** is treated by the canonical theory as a possible
   reconstruction horizon, not as a denied physical event.
2. **The Hubble Tension** may include a residue of the observer-world interface;
   this is a working hypothesis, not a completed explanation.
3. **Portability:** The Pantheon+ and BAO checks suggest a potentially portable
   correction structure, but the cross-probe evidence is still exploratory.

**Working Verdict:** The data suggest map-level stresses worth formalizing.
AOC provides a language for testing whether those stresses are observer-bound
residues, calibration artifacts, or genuine cosmological deviations.
