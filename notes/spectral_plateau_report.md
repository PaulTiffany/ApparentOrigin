# Spectral Plateau Report: The Saturated Observer

## Summary
We measured a **Spectral Information Volume** diagnostic (log-determinant of
the precision matrix) for the Pantheon+ pipeline across a redshift sweep. This
is a candidate grounding for "Observer Capacity" $\Omega_O$.

## Results
- **Active Growth Exponent ($z \le 0.5$):** $p_{spec} \approx 0.43$.
- **Saturation Point:** $z \approx 0.5$.
- **Information Floor:** For $z > 0.5$, the gain in independent modes is near zero.

## Interpretation: The Saturated Atlas
The pipeline's "Information Altitude" does not appear to grow linearly with
redshift in this diagnostic. Instead, it shows a candidate saturation region.
1. **Low Redshift:** Adding supernovae effectively builds the local atlas ($p_{spec} \approx 0.43$).
2. **High Redshift ($z > 0.5$):** The new data points are so sparse and noisy that they fail to create new independent modes in the global covariance. They are "swallowed" by the existing atlas.

## Impact on the Hubble Tension
In the previous $H_0$ prediction, we assumed $\Omega_O$ continued to grow with
$N$. This diagnostic challenges that assumption: the Pantheon+ pipeline appears
to hit a **Spectral Plateau** near $z \approx 0.5$.

If the plateau survives controls, the "Observer Altitude" of the SNIa pipeline
may be effectively limited near $z=0.5$ capacity. In that framing, the CMB
bridge is not simply a bridge from $z=0$ to $z=1100$; it is a bridge from a
candidate saturated SNIa atlas to a high-redshift CMB atlas.

## Grounded Prediction Update
The mismatch may be larger than a row-count model suggests. The SNIa pipeline
may have less effective capacity than its sample size implies.
- **Old $\Omega_{SN}$:** 1550
- **New $\Omega_{SN}$ (Saturated Modes):** $\approx 1400$ (from $n_{eff}$ at $z=2.5$)

This candidate grounding moves the model closer to the observed tension by
reducing the assumed capacity of the local probe, but it still requires survey
handover and covariance controls.
