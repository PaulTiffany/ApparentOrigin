# Exploratory Note: The Density Catastrophe

## The Paradox of the Thick Observer
Individual Pantheon+ supernovae appear "thick" ($p \approx 0.05$), meaning their individual quality is nearly constant across redshift. This initially suggested a high-dynamic range observer.

## The Catastrophe Hypothesis
The independent "catastrophe" view is useful: **Aggregate Reconstruction
Power** can degrade at high redshift even when individual object quality stays
high. In the Pantheon+ binning used here, **Sample Density** ($n$) drops from
$>1000$ to $1$.

### Aggregate Scaling
If we define the pipeline's reconstruction uncertainty as $\sigma_{agg} \approx \sigma / \sqrt{n}$, the exponent $p$ jumps from $0.05$ to **$\approx 3.3$**. 

- **Low-z ($z \approx 0.1$):** $\sigma_{agg} \approx 0.005$
- **High-z ($z \approx 2.3$):** $\sigma_{agg} \approx 0.210$

This is a **40-fold increase** in reconstruction error over a small redshift range. That is the "catastrophe."

## Structural Insight: The Shannon Horizon
The "Apparent Origin" mechanism in AOC may have a sampling-density analogue: a
**Sampling Horizon**.
- **Adaptedness** requires a minimum data density to sustain the symbolic connection $A_O$.
- When $n \to 1$ per resolution element, the atlas may fracture.
- The Big Bang analogy should be treated cautiously: in the canonical theory,
  the Big Bang boundary is a possible reconstruction horizon, not something
  supported by this Pantheon+ sampling note.

## Strategic North Star
AOC must distinguish between **Object-Level Thickness** and **Atlas-Level Density**. 
- Hubble-tension-like behavior may partly arise from trying to extend a
  high-density local atlas into a sparse high-redshift regime.
- The "Transition Map" between these regimes must account for the **Density Catastrophe**.

## Science of the Interface (Updated)
The interface hypothesis is that apparent floors can occur when our **capacity
to resolve a continuous manifold** degrades because sampling density hits the
Nyquist-like limit of the reconstruction pipeline.
- **Candidate prediction:** A cosmological probe may show an apparent
  reconstruction floor at the redshift where its sampling density $n(z)$ falls
  below an adaptedness threshold such as $s_O^{-3}$.
