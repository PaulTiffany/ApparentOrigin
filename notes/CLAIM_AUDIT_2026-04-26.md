## Useful New Threads

The new notes and scripts contain several potentially useful research threads:

1. **Atlas stitching:** low- and high-redshift partitions may prefer different
   effective parameters or residual corrections.
2. **Spectral plateau / density collapse:** information volume and sampling
   density may saturate or degrade near a transition region.
3. **BAO portability:** a SNIa-derived residue was tested against a small BAO
   summary set.
4. **Information pressure:** an attempt to relate information-flow gradients to
   an effective `H0` residue.
5. **Two-pipeline simulation:** controlled forward modeling of apparatus-bound
   `K` gives the cleanest internal support for the formal `K` mechanism.
6. **v1 proof-derived deformation:** a pre-registered power-law deformation
   derived from the apparatus-bound `K` program reduces the specification
   flexibility of the v0 log-threshold probe, while remaining conditional on
   already-seen Pantheon+ data and prechosen toy exponents.

## Overclaim Risks

The following claims should be treated as noncanonical until separately
derived, preregistered, and externally validated:

1. "The Hubble Tension is solved."
2. "AOC is established for the late universe."
3. "The BAO result proves universality."
4. "The Pantheon+ correction proves the atlas fracture."
5. "AOC provides the unique mathematical key."
6. "The Big Bang is the Shannon Horizon."
7. ">4 sigma" language for correction gains without a fully specified model
   comparison, parameter accounting, and look-elsewhere penalty.

## Specific Concerns

### Step-function or transition-map corrections

Several analyses apply a boundary or smooth transition around `z ~= 0.5`.
This may be physically meaningful, but it may also encode survey transitions,
sample-density changes, calibration handovers, or choices discovered after
looking at Pantheon+.

Canonical phrasing:

> The `z ~= 0.5` transition is a candidate information-geometric fracture or
> pipeline handover region requiring controls.

Do not say:

> The `z ~= 0.5` transition proves a universal cosmological law.

### BAO portability

The BAO script uses a small eBOSS consensus summary and a transported
SNIa-derived residue. This is interesting as a sanity check, but not enough to
claim cross-probe proof.

Canonical phrasing:

> A SNIa-derived residue produced an exploratory improvement on a small BAO
> consistency check.

Do not say:

> BAO proves AOC universality.

### Information pressure

The information-pressure mechanism currently includes a chosen scale factor in
the mapping from information flow to `H0`. Until that coupling is derived or
predeclared, accuracy claims are vulnerable to curve-fitting criticism.

Canonical phrasing:

> Information-flow gradients are a candidate mechanism for an effective
> expansion-rate residue.

Do not say:

> `H0` has been derived as the conjugate variable to information flow.

   additional same-dataset Pantheon+ sharpening.
