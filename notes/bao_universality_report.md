# BAO Portability Report: Residue Transfer Check

## Summary
We performed an exploratory cross-probe residue-transfer check. We applied the
residue measured in the SNIa pipeline to a small independent eBOSS BAO consensus
summary.

## Results
- **Residue Applied:** $-2.23\%$ distance shift (derived from SNIa $z > 0.5$).
- **Standard Consistency (Planck Anchor):** $\chi^2 = 34.03$.
- **AOC Consistency (Planck Anchor):** $\chi^2 = 28.48$.
- **Consistency Gain ($\Delta \chi^2$):** **$+5.54$** in this simplified
  consistency diagnostic.

## Interpretation: Portability Candidate
This is an interesting portability check, not a proof of universality.
1. The candidate **Atlas Fracture** residue measured near $z \approx 0.5$ in
   Pantheon+ (SNIa) improves this simplified BAO consistency calculation.
2. Applying the same correction to a different probe improves consistency with
   the chosen CMB/Planck anchor in this setup.
3. This argues that the residue-transfer idea deserves a stricter BAO analysis
   with full covariance, parameter accounting, and a predeclared correction.

## Grounding the "Science of the Interface"
This result keeps the cross-probe hypothesis alive. It does not change AOC's
status from exploratory. The appropriate next step is a full BAO contract:
data provenance, covariance, nuisance parameters, predeclared correction, and
allowed/forbidden claims.

**Conclusion:** The SNIa-derived transition residue may be portable to BAO in
this simplified check. That is enough to motivate a stricter follow-up, not
enough to claim that the Hubble tension has been explained.
