# Residue Evolution Report: Partition Drift

## Summary
We mapped a candidate **Atlas Fracture** diagnostic by sliding the redshift
boundary $z_{split}$ across the Pantheon+ dataset. This shows how an internal
tension metric behaves as a function of the chosen partition.

## Results
- **Coherent Regime ($z < 0.4$):** The Local and Extended atlases are in relatively good agreement ($\Delta H_0 \approx 0$).
- **The Fracture Boundary ($z \approx 0.5$):** As we hit the **Spectral Plateau**, the tension begins to grow rapidly.
- **Large Drift ($z \approx 0.5 \to 0.7$):** The partition diagnostic peaks at
  $\Delta H_0 \approx -7$ km/s/Mpc.
- **The Flip ($z > 0.8$):** At very high redshift, the sample density ($n$) collapses so completely that the "Extended" atlas loses all geometrical stiffness and the tension flips sign.

## Scientific Interpretation: The Gradient of Ignorance
The Hubble-tension-like mismatch may be better studied as a **gradient** across
partitions rather than as one fixed scalar.
1. When the observer has high resolution and high density ($z < 0.4$), the "Standard Model" (the local atlas) is consistent.
2. The moment the pipeline hits its **Information Dynamic Range** limit ($z \approx 0.5$), the transition map fails.
3. The resulting **Drift** in $H_0$ and $\Omega_m$ is compatible with the
   adaptedness framing: the fitted connection may become curved when the
   observer can no longer maintain the same reconstruction structure.

## Conclusion: The Horizon as Process Hypothesis
This diagnostic suggests that apparent boundaries may behave like processes of
fracture that begin when sampling density degrades, rather than like a single
hard wall. The connection to the Big Bang remains an analogy from the canonical
observer-quotient theory, not a result of this Pantheon+ analysis.

**Grounding Verdict:** The residue map is a useful empirical diagnostic: it
shows tension growth near the candidate information-volume plateau. It supports
further pipeline-geometry work without establishing the cosmological
interpretation.
