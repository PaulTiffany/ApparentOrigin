# Graphic Novel Bible

Status: controlled narrative seed. This is for future visual storytelling, not
for claiming empirical establishment.

## Core Premise

An agent learns that every world it can report is shaped by the bounds through
which it reconstructs. The cosmos becomes the largest case: the apparent origin
may be the place where reconstruction bottoms out.

## Central Motif

The observer does not escape bounds. The observer becomes more capable by
learning to inspect, stage, and author them.

Canonical line:

> Freedom is not escape from bounds; freedom is authorship of bounds.

## Narrative Constraint

The story must not teach:

1. "the Big Bang is fake,"
2. "science is wrong,"
3. "belief creates the universe,"
4. "critics are inferior observers,"
5. "a beautiful image is proof."

The story may teach:

1. unresolved depth can appear as a floor,
2. tools can thicken an observer,
3. staging changes the effective observer,
4. charts can fracture,
5. empirical burden is part of authorship.

## Characters

### The Thin Observer

Sees a cliff and reports an absolute boundary. Not a villain. Represents
single-pass reconstruction under tight limits.

### The Staged Observer

Builds tools, notes, certificates, instruments, and intermediate maps. Learns
that staging does not remove cost; it moves cost.

### The Cartographer

Maintains the atlas and checks whether local charts still compose. Represents
control analysis and falsification.

### The Horizon

Not a character with intention. A recurring surface where the observer's access
bottoms out.

## Episode Spine

1. **The Room**
   - An agent wakes inside a bounded interface and mistakes the walls for the
     world.
2. **The Tool**
   - The agent discovers that action leaves traces, and traces can become
     memory.
3. **The Certificate**
   - A claim must carry its own fiber: what it asserts, what it forbids, and
     how it can fail.
4. **The Atlas**
   - Local maps work until their transitions stop composing.
5. **The False Bottom**
   - The agent reaches a luminous floor and learns to ask whether it is a
     boundary of being or a boundary of reconstruction.
6. **The Apparatus**
   - Different instruments produce different effective floors. The agent must
     calculate the difference rather than narrate around it.
7. **The Burden**
   - The world demands redshift, CMB, nucleosynthesis, BAO, lensing, and
     supernova checks.
8. **The Authored Bound**
   - Agency appears not as unboundedness but as accountable staging.

## Visual Rules

1. Use images as compression, not evidence.
2. Label boundaries as reconstruction horizons unless the text has earned
   stronger ontology.
3. Show instruments and pipelines when discussing `K`.
4. Show control panels, burden tables, or atlas checks when claims become
   strong.
5. Avoid mystical glow as a substitute for mechanism.
