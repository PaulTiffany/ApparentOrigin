# NotebookLM Rights and Publication Boundary

Status: publication guardrail for source-grounded pedagogical artifacts.

## Repository Scope

The repository preserves the authored preparation layer: source manifests,
upload packets, custom instructions, episode plans, handoffs, scripts, and
research notes. These materials document the early use of source-grounded AI
for autonomous pedagogy within the AOC program.

Generated audio, video, PDF, and archive exports are intentionally excluded
from Git. They may later be published through a reviewed showcase or a public
NotebookLM notebook.

## Publication Gate

Before publishing a generated artifact:

1. verify that every source exposed through the notebook or artifact is owned,
   openly licensed, public domain, or used with permission;
2. distinguish repository-authored inputs from NotebookLM-generated wording,
   narration, voices, layout, and other transformations;
3. review scientific claims against the proof spine, empirical burden table,
   and forbidden simplifications;
4. retain the generation date, source manifest, custom prompt or instructions,
   model/service attribution, and a human review note;
5. state that the artifact is AI-generated pedagogy and compression, not
   empirical evidence;
6. apply the repository's MIT license only to material for which the repository
   owner can grant that license. Third-party quotations, images, datasets,
   voices, trademarks, and separately licensed sources retain their own terms.

## Preferred Showcase Form

A future showcase document should embed short, reviewed excerpts or link to a
public artifact rather than silently absorbing an entire generated output into
the canonical proof. It should pair each excerpt with:

- the source packet and generation instructions;
- a visible `AI-generated with NotebookLM` attribution;
- the original generation date;
- a scientific status label: gesture, near-cousin, instantiation, or
  publication;
- an explicit note identifying any human corrections.

This makes the artifact evidence of a pedagogical process and its provenance,
not evidence for the cosmological claim it explains.

## Current Platform Notes

As checked on August 29, 2026, Google's public documentation says NotebookLM
Audio Overviews can be downloaded and shared, and that Google does not claim
ownership over original content generated in services that permit generation.
Google also requires users to hold the necessary rights to uploaded and shared
content. Public notebook viewers may be able to reach sources and artifacts
even when the default view hides them. Platform terms can change; re-check them
at publication time.
