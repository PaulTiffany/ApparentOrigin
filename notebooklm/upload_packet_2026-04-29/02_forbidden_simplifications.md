# Forbidden Simplifications

Status: guardrail document for agents, media tools, explainers, and public copy.

Apparent-Origin Cosmology is easy to flatten into claims it does not make. Do
not generate or approve copy that says or implies the following unless a future
document explicitly proves it.

## Do Not Say

1. "The Big Bang is fake."
2. "There was no hot dense early universe."
3. "Cosmic expansion is only an illusion."
4. "`LambdaCDM` is obviously wrong."
5. "AOC already explains the Hubble tension."
6. "The CMB is just a projection artifact."
7. "The universe is a simulation boundary."
8. "The false bottom proves there is a deeper physical universe below the Big
   Bang."
9. "Critics reject AOC because they are thin observers."
10. "The image is evidence."

## Use Instead

1. "The Big Bang boundary may be an apparent-origin surface."
2. "The earliest reconstructible boundary is not automatically the ontological
   beginning."
3. "AOC preserves the empirical burden of standard cosmology."
4. "The current FRW chart is interpretive until `K` is derived or estimated."
5. "Pipeline-dependent reconstruction floors are a possible long-term empirical
   handle."
6. "The image is a compression of the intuition, not evidence."
7. "Boundary of reconstruction, not beginning of being."

## Critic Discipline

The framework predicts that some observers will refuse recruitment into an
integration. That prediction must not be used as a shortcut around empirical
burden.

Operational rule:

> Criticism routes first through burden, controls, and falsification. Only after
> that may observer-thickness be discussed.

## Media Discipline

Every public-facing asset should preserve these constraints:

1. Standard observations remain binding.
2. The Big Bang is treated as a possible reconstruction horizon, not dismissed.
3. AOC is not empirically established.
4. The `K` parameter is the load-bearing next problem.
5. The false-bottom metaphor is subordinate to the formal apparent-origin
   framework.
