# Glossary

Status: canonical vocabulary for this repository.

## Apparent-Origin Cosmology

The research program studying whether the earliest reconstructible cosmological
boundary should be interpreted as an observer-bounded reconstruction horizon
rather than an automatic ontological beginning.

## Apparent-Origin Surface

A boundary in an observer's reconstruction that appears origin-like because
unresolved depth has been quotiented into an extremal equivalence class.

## False Bottom

Public metaphor for an apparent-origin surface. Use carefully. It does not mean
"the Big Bang is fake." It means the reconstruction may bottom out before being
bottoms out.

## Reconstruction Horizon

Physics-facing term for the deepest coherent surface available to a bounded
observer or observation pipeline.

## Observer-Bounded Reconstruction

A map from an underlying process space to an observer's reconstruction space
under finite resolution, memory, integration bandwidth, instrumentation, and
model capacity.

## Observer Quotient

The equivalence relation induced when distinctions below the observer threshold
cannot be maintained. Unresolved variation is represented as an equivalence
class rather than as further depth.

## Observer Thickness

The effective capacity of an observer to preserve, stage, and integrate
distinctions before emitting a reconstruction.

Thin observers expose boundaries sharply. Thick observers amortize boundaries
across memory, embodiment, instruments, tools, culture, retrieval, scratchpads,
or staged computation.

Canonical sentence:

> Observer thickness determines whether a boundary appears as a cliff or as
> smooth judgment.

## Amortization

The relocation of reconstruction cost from an exposed act into prior
integration, memory, tooling, instrumentation, culture, or staged computation.

Canonical sentence:

> Amortization does not eliminate the obstruction; it pays the cost elsewhere.

## Atlas Fracture

The failure mode where an observer's local reconstruction charts no longer
compose coherently under drift. Apparent-origin claims are legitimate only when
the reconstruction atlas remains coherent up to the boundary.

## K Parameter

The finite reconstruction budget used in the first FRW observer-quotient chart.
In that chart, `K` bounds the access cost:

```tex
\kappa_O^{access}(t) \le K.
```

Until `K` is derived or estimated from apparatus, coarse-graining, or a physical
bound, AOC remains interpretive rather than predictive.

## Apparatus-Bound K

The safest current framing of `K`: the effective budget of a concrete
instrument and inference pipeline.

## RG-Effective K

A possible future framing in which `K` runs under coarse-graining. This remains
a placeholder until a real running equation is derived.

## Fundamental K

A possible future framing in which `K` is constrained by fundamental physical
capacity, such as entropy or holographic bounds. This is deferred.

## Feasibility Band

The range where an observer has enough reliable access to operate, instrument,
compose, and analyze without pretending to be unbounded.

Canonical sentence:

> Compose only where the operator remains measurable.

## Authorship of Bounds

The agency reading of the framework: an agent is not free because it lacks
constraints, but because it can inspect, revise, stage, and author the bounds
under which it reconstructs.
