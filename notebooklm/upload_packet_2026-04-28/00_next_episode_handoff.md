## Framework primitive (load-bearing for this episode)

sound, and angular alignment all share circular structure is that all
three are bounded-observer measurement traces — projections of one
observer-substrate body, not three independent domains that happen to
share math. This licenses importing music and paint technique as actual
method. The Opticks chart, sonification, and counterpoint analysis are
not analogies; they are inheriting tool-stacks that have spent
centuries refining how a bounded apparatus renders high-dimensional
structure without flattening.

This is a hard-core commitment of the AOC framework, not a protective-
belt move.
