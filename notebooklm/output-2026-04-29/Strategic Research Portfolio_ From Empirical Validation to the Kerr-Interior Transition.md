#### 2\. Empirical Calibration: The Sprint D Sim-Null Analysis

Following the validation of the prediction contract, research progressed to  **Sprint D** , an instantiation-class calibration of the Episode 2 findings. While Episode 2 identified visual anomalies in pipeline behavior, Sprint D provides the statistical scaffolding required to test these findings against  $\\Lambda$ CDM. By running 1000 realizations through a surrogate operator-noise model, the program has moved beyond simple observation into formal significance testing against the standard cosmological model.

##### Headline Results: 1000-Realization  $\\Lambda$ CDM Sweep

Analysis of the low-ell sweep under  $\\Lambda$ CDM yields the following critical data points:

* **$\\ell=3**$  **Parallel-Fifths Anomaly:**  In the primary noise model (1.0x),  **0/1000 realizations**  reached the observed all-six-pair lockstep configuration (100th percentile).  
* **Noise Sensitivity:**  At a 0.5x noise scale—the scale where surrogate rotation-axis dispersion most closely matches the observed \~12° dispersion from Episode 2—the lockstep rate reached only  **1.4%**  (98.6th percentile).  
* **Mask Contribution:**  Mask geometry (galcut20) alone produced a  **7x increase**  in lockstep events compared to the noise-only baseline. While masking is a meaningful driver of correlated behavior, it is statistically insufficient to reproduce the observed 6/6 pattern.  
* **$\\ell=2**$  **Consistency:**  In contrast to the  $\\ell=3$  findings, the  $\\ell=2$  results (2/6 pairs) remained within the  **"null bulk,"**  confirming that the headline anomaly is unique to the octupole.The "So What?" of these findings lies in the widening gap between current cosmological modeling and observation. While  $\\Lambda$ CDM simulations using surrogate pipelines show that mask geometry can induce  *some*  lockstep motion, they fail to reproduce the total parallel-fifths configuration observed in the actual Planck data. This suggests that the Pipeline Independence Postulate overestimates the autonomy of current extraction algorithms. These visual and statistical anomalies necessitate the use of alternate sensory channels to detect structural residues that visual charts obscure.
