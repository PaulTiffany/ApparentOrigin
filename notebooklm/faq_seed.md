# FAQ Seed

Status: canonical answers for NotebookLM Q&A generation.

## Is AOC saying the Big Bang is fake?

No. AOC asks whether the Big Bang boundary should be interpreted as an
observer-bounded reconstruction horizon rather than an automatic ontological
beginning. It does not deny the observations modeled by standard cosmology.

## Does AOC deny cosmic expansion?

No. AOC does not begin by denying cosmic expansion. It asks how bounded
observers reconstruct origin, distance, expansion history, and boundary
conditions.

## Does AOC deny the CMB?

No. The CMB remains a load-bearing observation. AOC must eventually preserve,
reinterpret, or predict controlled deviations in CMB-related observables.

## What is an apparent-origin surface?

An apparent-origin surface is a boundary in an observer's reconstruction that
appears origin-like because unresolved depth has been quotiented into an
extremal equivalence class.

## What is the false bottom?

The false bottom is the public metaphor for an apparent-origin surface. It means
the reconstruction bottoms out. It does not mean reality has been proven to
bottom out there, and it does not mean standard cosmology is fake.

## What is the observer quotient?

The observer quotient is the compression induced when a bounded observer cannot
maintain distinctions below its resolution, memory, or integration threshold.
Those distinctions are represented as an equivalence class rather than as
further depth.

## What does the FRW chart show?

The first FRW chart shows that if observer access is represented by
`Omega_O(t)=a(t)^2`, and access cost is `1/a(t)`, then a finite reconstruction
budget `K` produces a minimum reconstructible time `t_K`. Earlier times are
collapsed into a boundary class for that observer.

## What does the FRW chart not show?

It does not show that LambdaCDM is false. It does not derive the access-cost law
natively from cosmology. It does not yet determine `K`. It does not yet compare
to observational data.

## Why is K important?

`K` sets the apparent-origin surface. If `K` is arbitrary, the surface is
arbitrary. If `K` is calculable from apparatus, coarse-graining, or physical
capacity, AOC can begin making operational predictions.

## Does AOC explain the Hubble tension?

No. The current framework does not claim to explain the Hubble tension. The
safe statement is that apparatus-bound `K` has the same broad shape as
pipeline-dependent inference. Any real comparison would require predicting sign
and order of magnitude before fitting to data.

## What is observer thickness?

Observer thickness is the effective capacity of an observer to preserve, stage,
and integrate distinctions before reconstruction. Thin observers expose
boundaries sharply. Thick observers amortize boundaries across memory, tools,
instruments, culture, or staged computation.

## Does calling someone a thin observer answer criticism?

No. That is a failure mode. Criticism routes first through burden, controls, and
falsification. Only after that may observer-thickness be discussed.

## Are the images evidence?

No. The images are visual compression. They help communicate the observer
quotient and apparent-origin intuition, but they do not prove the cosmology.

## What is the next technical task?

The next load-bearing task is apparatus-bound `K`: build a concrete observation
or inference pipeline where the reconstruction budget can be estimated, then
derive the corresponding `t_K`.
